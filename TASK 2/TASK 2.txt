Test Email:
From: support@micros0ft-secure.com
Subject: Urgent: Account Verification Required
Body:
Dear Customer,

We noticed unusual activity in your account. To avoid suspension, please verify your details immediately by clicking the link below:

http://secure-login.microsoft.verify-account.com

Failure to act within 24 hours will result in account termination.

Thank you,
Microsoft Security Team

1.Sender’s Email Address (Spoofing)
- Appears to be from support@micros0ft-secure.com.
- Notice the subtle typo: “micros0ft” with a zero instead of “o”. Classic spoofing tactic.

2.Email Headers (Discrepancies)
- Using a header analyzer (e.g., Microsoft Message Header Analyzer), you’d see the Return-Path doesn’t match the “From” domain.
- SPF/DKIM/DMARC checks likely fail.

3.Suspicious Links or Attachments
- Link points to verify-account.com, not an official Microsoft domain.
- Hovering reveals mismatch between displayed text and actual URL.

4.Urgent or Threatening Language
- “Failure to act within 24 hours will result in account termination.”
- Creates panic to push immediate action.

5.Mismatched URLs
- Displayed as “secure-login.microsoft” but actually redirects to verify-account.com.

6.Spelling/Grammar Errors
- “micros0ft” domain typo.
- Body uses awkward phrasing: “Failure to act…” instead of professional wording.

7.Suspicious Attachments
- In this sample, no attachment, but phishing emails often include malicious PDFs or Word files.
